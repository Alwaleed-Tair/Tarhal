# Hotel photos: credits

(name.webp, name-2x.webp) used by the pages. All from Wikimedia Commons under a free licence.
The hotels in the app are samples: these are fitting room photos chosen by room type, not photos of those hotels.
The Commons file names mention the places where they were taken; the app does not show those names.
